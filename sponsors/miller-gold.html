Miller Gold & Co

Miller Gold House (Pty) Ltd · est. 1992 · FICA registered

Miller Gold & Co (Miller Gold House (Pty) Ltd) was established in 1992 by Sindy Miller, a GIA and Jewellery Council SA jewellery professional with 34 years' industry experience.

The house specialises in premium new and pre-owned jewellery, vintage and antique jewellery, natural diamonds, gemstones, watches, Krugerrands, engagement rings, valuations and bespoke / design-a-ring work.

Every showcased piece is specifically selected for uniqueness, quality and authenticity. Pre-owned jewellery and gold coins are authenticated by expert gemologists. Miller Gold is FICA registered and FICA compliant.

Miller Gold is primarily an online retailer, not a walk-in showroom. Private viewing and collection are by appointment in Cape Town, Sandton / Johannesburg and Durban.
