# HERO4 housing path (secondary)

Smaller and often cheaper on the used market. Use if H9–13 shells are scarce or you want a smaller puck.

## Camera / cavity proxy

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Camera body | ~**59 × 41 × 30 mm** | Commonly listed 41×59×30 |
| Max insert (recommended) | **≤ 57 × 39 × 28 mm** | ~1 mm/side margin |
| Approx volume | ~**62 cm³** | ~half of H9–13 |
| Housing outer (PCMag) | ~65 × 67 × 35 mm | 2.55×2.62×1.37 in |
| Standard depth rating | 40 m | Standard Housing |
| Dive Housing | 60 m | Slightly different shell |

## BacPac depth hack

LCD / Battery BacPac used a **deeper backdoor** on the same front shell.

- Waterproof BacPac backdoor: still ~40 m  
- Extra rear depth: typically on the order of **~15–20 mm** (confirm with calipers on a BacPac door)  
- Use this if battery thickness is the blocker, not PCB area

## When to prefer H4

- Ultra-cheap scrap housings  
- Smaller boat classes / less mass  
- Early RF prototypes where volume is secondary  

## When to prefer H9–13

- Primary Boat Puck product path  
- Room for L1/L5 antenna + RTK module + pack + radio  
- Current GoPro mount ecosystem / clones in production
