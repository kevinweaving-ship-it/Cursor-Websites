## Must-win vs Atlas (product claim)

1. **Same race start tools** (DTL/TTL/timer) at a fraction of cost.  
2. **Stronger positioning** when committee RTK is up (HALO-class on the instrument).  
3. **Same fleet network** as dinghy pucks — one club system.
