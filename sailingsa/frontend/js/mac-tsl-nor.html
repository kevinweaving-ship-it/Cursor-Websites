NOR and Sailing Instructions

SASWC Team Racing Regional Championship

Venue: Milnerton Aquatic Club (MAC)
Date: 17 & 18 October 2026

Notice is hereby given that the above Sailing Event is due to take place as detailed in this Notice of Race. Entries are invited in terms of this Notice.

1. Registration

Invitation will take place online through the TSL@MAC WhatsApp group. All teams must register their crews at SA Sailing WC Team Sailing Event by Sunday 11 October 2026. A list of participants, their gender and development status, must be finalised before the regatta starts on Saturday 18 October 2026.

2. Skipper’s briefing

There will be a compulsory briefing at the MAC Clubhouse on Saturday 17 October at 09:00. All races to be completed by 16:00 Sunday afternoon.

3. Organizing authority

The challenge will be organised by the SASWC Representative.

4. Rules and compliance

The challenge will be governed by the rules as defined in the Racing Rules of Sailing for 2025 to 2028 (RRS).

5. Sailing Instructions

Sailing Instructions will be available from the Race Officer on request, on completion of registration formalities, and displayed on the official regatta notice board in the main clubhouse.

The course will be laid out in either an “N” course with separate Start and Finish lines or a “Square” course with Start and Finish on the same line.

Starts will follow a 3-2-1-START minute countdown sequence.

6. Race records and reporting

All team managers and sailors will be required to enter their bios (club, gender, race and age) on SA Sailing WC Team Sailing Event by Sunday 11 October 2026. This is needed for reporting back to SAS as part of the Talent Nurturing Program for funding requirements.

7. Scrutiny

The boats are the TSL 410s and are one-design for team racing. All boats will be rigged and maintained by the Boat Manager. Rig adjustments can be done prior to races. Should any boat suffer damage or have a maintenance issue during the race, the race will be abandoned and restarted. The RO or Umpire must be informed immediately.

8. Safety

A team is entirely responsible for its own safety — afloat or ashore — and nothing reduces that responsibility. The team manager must always be aware of conditions and act accordingly.

Every contestant (or guardian if underage) is responsible for signing an Indemnity form.

A personal flotation device with whistle must be worn by all sailors.

9. Team

Double-handed races on TSL dinghies. 3 boats with 6 participants per team, with a team manager as a reserve participant. Crews may be switched and helms and crews inter-changed within a team, but no registered sailor may sail for any other team.

10. Team manager

Each team must have a Manager/Representative who will be required to sign off the completed races.

A team-racing guide will be forwarded to all managers for tips and training aids. Managers must ensure their teams are ready and on standby on shore for efficient switching between races.

11. Scoring

Contestants will compete on a 3-on-3 match race format with each position allocated as a point. The 3-boat team’s points are added and the team with the lowest score is declared the winner. Example: Team A finish 1st, 4th and 6th = 11 points; Team B finish 2nd, 3rd and 5th = 10 points — Team B wins. Teams will compete on a knockout basis.

The formula for a round-robin event is: races = (number of teams) × (number of teams − 1) / 2. This means that 8 teams require 28 round-robin races.

Should conditions be such that races cannot be completed, the RO will divide the fleet into 2 flights, with the winners and runners-up having a knock-out final race.
