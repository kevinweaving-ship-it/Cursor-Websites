# French entities

Greece named **ECO-VIE Association, based in France** as shipowner. The public Greek decisions do not print a French street, RNA number, or officer. The job is to identify *which* ECO-VIE, then who controlled it.

| RNA on JO record | blank |
| Officers on JO record | none |

**Why this is the lead.** The name matches Greece’s “ECO-VIE Association”. The seat is the same hamlet later used by Bruce’s SCI. Other French ECO-VIE / E.C.O-V.I.E. associations exist (Fontenay-le-Comte 2002; Tourrettes-sur-Loup RNA `W061001187` from 2009; others). Those are **different entities**. Tourrettes-sur-Loup is inland of Nice and thematically similar; it is **not** the one with a documented Bruce address overlap.

**Still missing:** founding declaration, statutes, officer list, and any later changes, from the Var association registry (`pref-associations@var.gouv.fr`). Without those, Bruce cannot be listed as president, treasurer, or signatory of ECO-VIE.

## 5. How to read “sale vs transfer”

Three consistent pictures, none closed:

1. **Bruce remained the beneficial owner** and parked legal title in ECO-VIE (association at his documented address), then registered the hull in Poland.
2. **Bruce transferred the boat to people who used that association**, and Greece is chasing the association because that is what the Polish certificate named.
3. **Greece served the wrong ECO-VIE.** Unlikely given the name, but the service file is the check.

The Greek decisions call ECO-VIE the **πλοιοκτήτρια εταιρεία** (“shipowning company”) even though a loi 1901 association is not a commercial company. That is the Port Fund’s wording, not a French legal classification.
