## 2. Mooring ball → concrete jetty — **partial; two stories, only one is Sunrise-specific**

**Established location on 18 Dec 2023:** already against the cracked concrete quay **inside** the port, chain to the wall, French boat on the outer side. Coast Guard: made fast, parallel, inside Vlycho harbour.

**Sunrise-specific move:** residents, same day: found tied at the **mole** one April morning, no name, no owner; “they brought it here to sink.” That is a **tow to the wall to die**, not a storm report. Year still unnamed. Family: she had been on a **mooring ball** before that.

**Useful local practice (not a file on this hull):**

- Vlycho has **both** laid moorings / buoys off the quay **and** alongside berths on the town quay. Cruiserswiki (snippet; page CF-walled here): “numerous laid up boats and (frankly) **wrecks alongside** here amongst the smart yachts.” Mooring buoys just off the quay by arrangement with Vliho Yacht Club.
- Katabatic gusts and dragging onto the lee shore are real Ionian weather. Port Police warping a leaking unmanned hull to shallow quay so she does not block the channel is **plausible**. It is **not** written on Sunrise.
- Storm-drag and “they brought it here to sink” are **different** stories. Do not merge them.

**Reject:** “October 17, 2025 iLefkada.gr — already at that exact spot by the quay for roughly five years.” **No such article found.** The dated iLefkada line is **18 Dec 2023**: more than **two** years in the **bay** (κόλπος), already taking on water, listed **that day**. “Bay” is not “quay”. Five years would push arrival to ~2020; that remains **open**, not proved by this dump.

**Street View now dates the quay (21 Nov 2026 screenshots).** Google, 105 Επαρ.Οδ. Λευκάδας–Βασιλικής:

## 5. Insurance claim after abandon + Polish flag + ECO-VIE — **possible; not found; Greeks are already stuck**

Score the user’s question, not the dump’s legal essay.

| Question | Score | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Did he abandon her in Vlycho? | **holds** | iLefkada >2 years, leaking; Hermanus Nov 2020–Apr 2022; locals: dumped to sink. |
| Could a hull policy still have been claimed as a sudden loss somewhere else? | **open** | No public claim. Policies are private. That is the file to demand. |
| Would a competent marine insurer **pay** a slow Vlycho rot as a fortuitous total loss? | **useful no** | Institute-type yacht clauses exclude wear, tear, gradual ingress, lack of due diligence. A surveyor looking at tarps, boom, and years of growth would call neglect. That is why a **clean** claim would need a different date/place/cause — which is the fraud theory, not a finding. |
| Does Polish reflag + ECO-VIE stop Greek wreck-removal? | **so far, yes in practice** | Greece served ECO-VIE; two 2026 auctions empty; wreck still there Aug 2024 (ZigZag) and after the empty tenders (Paleros). Law 2881/2001 still names the owner. A dormant 2008 association is a dry well. |
| Does that **erase** liability in law? | **no, not proved** | A later sham transfer can be pierced; we do not have the Polish history copy, so we do not know if ECO-VIE was owner **at** the casualty or only when Greece served in 2025. |
| Nairobi 300 GT compulsory wreck insurance / direct action on the P&I club? | **useful explanation** | A ~20 m wooden hull is almost certainly **under** 300 GT. That is why the Port Fund had to tender itself and chase ECO-VIE, not an insurer. Do not treat Nairobi as a closed legal opinion on this file. |
| DEKPA / TEPAI / insurance certificate on entry? | **open** | Ask Nydri whether she ever presented papers. An abandoned drop may never have. |

**Bottom line on fraud:** the public pattern is **abandonment + hard-to-sue owner + empty Greek process**. That is **consistent with** a liability dump. It is **not** a proved payout. “The port isn’t aware he claimed” cannot be tested from the web. Get the policy schedule and the notice of loss.
