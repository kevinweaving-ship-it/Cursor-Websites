# Hypothesis test — abandonment, jetty move, Polish flag, ECO-VIE, insurance

This is **not** a finding of fraud. It scores the story against public records. Insurance fraud is a crime; a pattern that *looks like* a liability dump is not a proved claim file.

Greek port / casualty / salvage file: [`GREECE.md`](GREECE.md).

Scores: **holds** / **partial** / **open** / **fails**.

## The story being tested

1. Both boats were already in Vlycho from **2021**.
2. Sunrise was **abandoned**, rotting, leaking over time — not lost on a voyage.
3. Someone **dragged her from a mooring to the concrete jetty** so she would not block other boats, then let her go down there.
4. **Polish flag and ECO-VIE** came in to cut Greek wreck-removal liability.
5. Insurers were **not told** she sat abandoned in Vlycho; a claim may have said she sank **somewhere else**.

**Partial on the drag.** Residents, quoted the same day:

That is **local testimony of a tow to the mole to die**, not a Coast Guard finding. It does **not** name the year. Photos on 18 Dec show **SUNRISE** on the bow, so “without name” means no papers / no master on arrival — not that the hull was anonymous in December.

If Bruce was in Hermanus from Nov 2020, the hull was already without him in the Med for long stretches. That **strengthens abandonment**. It does not by itself date the mole tow. The one Europe trip between Nov 2020 and Apr 2022 is the other window in which he could have moved her France → Vlycho, which would match iLefkada’s “in the bay more than two years” by Dec 2023.

A same-day drag on 18 Dec 2023 is **unlikely**: the Coast Guard was called because two boats already berthed in the port were going under.

**Mooring ball vs quay.** Family: she had been on a ball, then against the wall. **No public Maxar frame shows a 20 m hull on a Vlycho buoy.** Inner-bay mosaics: **2 Aug 2020** (France week) and **21 Oct 2022** are yacht-sized boats on balls only; **28 Aug 2025** has the wreck **on the pier**. See [`SATELLITE.md`](SATELLITE.md). Planet monthly mosaics (21 Nov 2026): **candidates** Dec 2020, Feb/May 2021, Feb/Apr 2022, last mooring-style **Jan 2023** — **not IDs**; the auto-screener missed the known wreck on the wall. Street View still closes the **wall**: empty May 2019, occupied May 2023. Oct 2022 she is on **neither** wall nor ball in this bay — that fights iLefkada’s “two years in the bay” unless the reporter over-estimated, she sat in another inlet, or a 3–5 m blob in other months is her. A same-day storm-drag to the quay on 18 Dec is **out**. Full Planet log: [`PLANET.md`](PLANET.md).

## 3. Which day she actually sank — **18 Dec is the official casualty; the rot is older**

Coast Guard (restated 19 Dec by Kathimerini / Rthess): **morning of 18 Dec 2023**, notified of two pleasure craft, Polish and French flags, moored parallel in Vlycho port. First hull **half-sunk from water ingress**; second **sunk from pressure** of the first. No injuries. Petroleum pollution. Boom. **Preliminary investigation opened under Penal Code 277 §1A (causing a shipwreck)** and PD 55/1998 (marine environment).

That is the day the State treated as the wreck event. It is **not** a survey of when the planks first leaked. A wooden hull left years on a quay can make water for a long time and then go in a night. The photos are already a wreck, not a boat that looked fine at breakfast.

Outcome of that 277 file: **not published**. Unlike the April 2024 UK boat, no arrest report was found for Sunrise.

## 4. Polish flag after abandonment, to duck the Greeks — **open, timing allows it**

Port Fund Appendix A (Decision **27/2026**, 31 Mar 2026) now independently prints **`POL000AYP`**, Polish flag, 20 m, wood. The number is established. The **date it was issued** is not.

| Bound | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Last public French berth | **5 Aug 2020**, Port Vauban (charter site) |
| REJA24 exists | **1 Aug 2020** |
| Polish flag on her | **18 Dec 2023** (Coast Guard) |
| Local “more than two years” in Vlycho | implies **~Dec 2021 or earlier** |

So she can have been **moved to Greece, left, then reflagged Polish while already a dead ship**. She can also have been reflagged in France in late 2020 and then sailed/towed to Vlycho. The Polish `odpis pełny` for `POL000AYP` is the only document that dates first Polish registration against those bounds.

A recreational Polish flag on an abandoned 20 m wooden hull in a Greek port is a **poor recovery target**: foreign register, no AIS found, owner later named as a French association. That is **consistent with** making Greek enforcement hard. It is not a signed statement of motive.

## 5. Moved into ECO-VIE to escape liability — **consistent pattern, transfer date unknown**

Greece’s 5 Sep 2025 notice names **ECO-VIE Association, France** as shipowner. Decision 133/2026 says it was served. Both 2026 auctions: **nobody came**. Law 2881/2001 puts removal on the owner. An association with no published assets is a dry well. Paleros Yacht Services still advertised the wreck in Vlycho after the empty tenders.

**ECO-VIE was not created for this wreck.** It was declared **27 Nov 2008** at Bastide des Planets — nine years before Sunrise Adventures Ltd, twelve years before REJA24. The Journal officiel has **no later modification, officer change, or dissolution**. INSEE has no SIREN. That is a **dormant 1901 association at Bruce’s hamlet**, not a 2023/24 wreck-dump company.

What remains open is the **date title moved into it** — before abandonment, after abandonment, or **after 18 Dec 2023**. A post-casualty amendment on the Polish register would still be the classic dump. Parking an already-dead hull in a 15-year-old empty association is the same wall with an older vehicle. Neither date is on the public web.

Until the Polish history copy and the Greek service file are in hand, call this **the working theory**, not a closed fact.

## 6. Insurance claim that she sank somewhere else — **fails on the public web; not disproved**

Searched: Bruce Weaving + insurance / assurance / sinistre / total loss / wreck; Sunrise Adventures; ECO-VIE; Vlycho. **No public claim, insurer, survey, or alternative casualty location.**

That does **not** mean no claim was filed. Hull policies and P&I files are private. If a claim exists, it will be in:

- the policy schedule (who was insured, from when, trading limits, laid-up warranty);
- the notice of loss (date, place, cause);
- the surveyor’s report;
- any “constructive total loss” correspondence.

A laid-up wreck in Vlycho from ~2021, then a later notice of loss at another position, would be the fraud. **There is no public document of that.** It is the first question for whoever holds the policy (French pleasure insurer, UK, or a broker from the Antibes years). SamBoat’s 2019 booking page implies the boat was offered commercially; commercial use often has a different policy than a private laid-up hull.

Do not treat “it smacks of insurance fraud” as proved. Treat it as **the file to demand**.

**Can he abandon, reflag Polish, park title in ECO-VIE, claim elsewhere, and leave the Greeks with nothing?** Publicly: abandonment **holds**; Polish-after-abandon **timing still open**; ECO-VIE as a dry well **fits the later Greek dead-end**; a paid claim **fails on the public web**. In practice the Port Fund has already **failed to get her lifted** (two empty auctions; ZigZag still photographed her at the quay **22 Aug 2024**). A ~20 m wooden hull is almost certainly under the Nairobi **300 GT** compulsory wreck-insurance threshold, which is why Greece is chasing the association rather than a P&I club. That is **useful** as to why the State looks powerless. It is not a finding that a claim was paid. Wear-and-tear / due-diligence exclusions would normally **kill** a slow-rot claim **if** the insurer saw Vlycho. A claim that she sank somewhere else is the only way that square closes — and that document is private.

## Bottom line

- **Abandonment in Vlycho for years:** holds. iLefkada: already leaking, then listed on 18 Dec. Family: Bruce in Hermanus from Nov 2020.
- **Down at the concrete jetty, not in the fairway:** holds for 18 Dec (photos + Coast Guard “inside the port”).
- **Towed to the mole one April to die:** local testimony, year and actor open.
- **18 Dec as the only sinking day:** official casualty date; the decay is older.
- **Polish flag:** number `POL000AYP` established on the Greek PDF. Date of issue still a window (Aug 2020–~Dec 2021).
- **ECO-VIE as a liability wall:** fits the later Greek dead-end. Association is **2008**, not a post-sinking incorporation. Transfer date unknown.
- **Insurance claim she sank somewhere else:** no public trace. Get the policy. Do not call that fraud on this file.
