## 3. Two different “tows” — do not merge them

### A. The April mole dump (how she got to this wall)

Residents, same Facebook thread aromalefkadas used:

> «Βρέθηκε αραγμένο στο **μόλο** εδώ στο Βλυχό, ένα πρωινό του **Απρίλη**, χωρίς όνομα και χωρίς αφεντικό, **το έφεραν εδώ για να βουλιάξει**, ο στόχος επετεύχθη.»

That is a **tow to the mole to die**. It is **local**. It does **not** name a tug, a skipper, or the year. “Without name” on arrival; by December **SUNRISE** is on the bow.

Family: she had been on a **mooring ball** first. Public Maxar of the whole bay: **no 20 m hull on a buoy** on **2 Aug 2020** or **21 Oct 2022**. Wall occupied by **May 2023**. Same-day storm-drag on **18 Dec is out**.

### B. The salvage tow in the tenders (never happened)

Every 2026 διακήρυξη lists the contractor’s job as:

> **Ανέλκυση, Ρυμούλκηση και απομάκρυνση** του πλοίου “SUNRISE” εκτός λιμένα Βλυχού

Lift, **tow**, remove **out of the harbour**. Highest bidder. That tow was **never awarded**. Two empty auctions. Wreck still on the wall July 2026.

## 4. Law the Port Fund used

**Law 2881/2001** (wreck removal): the **owner** is written to remove or destroy the wreck. If he does not, the port organisation may do the work as his agent, or **sell** the wreck by open auction.

Ministerial Decision **2123/34/01/19-10-2001** (ΦΕΚ 1439 Β): how that auction is run.

Classification on the tenders: **ναυάγιο και επικίνδυνο και επιβλαβές πλοίο** (wreck, dangerous and harmful vessel).
