# Bruce Sunrise project

Investigation file for **MV SUNRISE**, the converted Scottish wooden trawler Bruce Weaving publicly described as his Mediterranean charter boat, later recorded as a Polish-flagged wreck in Vlycho, Lefkada.

This is **not** a SailingSA production page. It is a sourced case file. Claims below are labelled **established**, **supported**, or **open**. Do not treat an open item as a finding.

Interactive summary: [MV Sunrise investigation canvas](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-12473912-9b36-4ad6-9586-5f790481e1ab/1ea0c96d-50d6-4b17-a6c7-0d76e15c7baa).

## Hypothesis (not a finding)

Long abandonment **holds**. Locals: dumped on the mole **one April morning** (year not in the quote). Family: Bruce in **Hermanus from Nov 2020**, one Europe trip in between, **left Hermanus for Europe April 2022** — that April is the leading dump candidate, not proved. They had already lived on the Var/Riviera for years (Roquebrune from 2001; Antibes/Nice charter 2018–2020). ECO-VIE is a **2008** association, not a post-sinking shell. No public insurance file. A later dump’s “five years at the quay” and several cruiser URLs **do not survive a retrieve** — see [`DIGEST.md`](DIGEST.md). Full story: [`CHRONOLOGY.md`](CHRONOLOGY.md). Scores: [`HYPOTHESIS.md`](HYPOTHESIS.md).

## What is still open

1. **Purchase date and seller** in Scotland (lead: Dougie Leask / Sunrise Charters, Stromness; hull lead *Sunbeam* / LK209).
2. **Who owned SUNRISE on 18 Dec 2023** (Polish `odpis pełny` for **`POL000AYP`**).
3. **Whether ECO-VIE of Roquebrune is the entity Greece served**, and who its officers were at transfer, sinking, and notice.
4. **Sale versus related-entity transfer** — same-address evidence is not a bill of sale.
5. **Exact Polish registration date** — only a window is proven (see [`POLAND_FLAG.md`](POLAND_FLAG.md)).
6. **Which April** she was put on the mole — quote unnamed; family travel (**Hermanus** Nov 2020 – Apr 2022) makes **April 2022** the leading candidate.
7. **Nydri Penal Code 277 file** outcome.
8. **Any hull / P&I policy and notice of loss** — not on the public web.
