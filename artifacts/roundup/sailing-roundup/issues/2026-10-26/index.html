Defending champion keeps the DF95 world crown at Theewater

Theewater Sports Club · 16–23 October 2026

The defending champion, Craig Richards, retained the title on 29 points — winning 7 of 11 qualifying races and 2 gold-fleet finals — ahead of Ulf Lindberg (45.4) and Odd Ornulf Stray (60).

Official listing: 71 skippers entered and 20 races completed at Theewater Sports Club.

Fact check: the older edition said 68 sailors and 20 races. The official entry list is 71; 68 skippers appear in the scored results. Races completed remain 20.

Best South African: Roy Gardener, 10th overall in Gold fleet. Michael Kavanagh was 2nd in Silver fleet (overall 19).
