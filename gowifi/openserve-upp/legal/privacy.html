Privacy Policy

GoWiFi Box · Effective 5 October 2026

GoWifi (Pty) Ltd, registration 2020/514776/07, 21 4th Avenue, Voelklip, Western Cape 7200, South Africa (“GoWiFi”, “we”) operates the GoWiFi Box application (“the App”). This generic policy explains how the App handles information. It is meant for staff use and for the Intuit developer listing.

1. Who this covers

The App is used by GoWiFi staff. It is not a public consumer app. It may process information about GoWiFi customers and suppliers that already sits in GoWiFi’s books and operations systems.

2. What we collect

Depending on what a staff member connects, the App may read:

  1. Invoice, payment, customer, and balance records from QuickBooks Online (GoWiFi’s own company file).
  2. Fibre order and service status from Openserve systems GoWiFi already uses.
  3. Staff login details needed to open the App.
  4. Technical logs (time of a request, success or failure) to keep the App working.

We do not ask the public to create an App account. We do not buy marketing lists for this App.

3. Why we use it

We use this information only to run GoWiFi: recreate invoice history, issue GoWiFi invoices and statements, see who is connected, and reconcile collections. Legal bases under POPIA are our legitimate business interest and, where needed, performing a contract with a customer.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with:

  1. GoWiFi staff who need it for accounts or support.
  2. Service providers we already use (for example Intuit / QuickBooks, hosting on GoWiFi’s own server in South Africa, and our bank or debit-order provider) so those services can do their job.
  3. Authorities if the law requires it.

5. Storage and security

Records are stored on GoWiFi’s server in South Africa and in the third-party systems named above. Access is limited to authorised staff. No method of transmission is perfectly secure.

6. How long we keep it

We keep billing and customer records for as long as needed to operate the account and to meet tax and legal retention rules, then delete or archive them in the ordinary course.

7. Your rights

If you are a customer or staff member whose information is in the App, you may ask GoWiFi for access, correction, or deletion where POPIA allows. Email accounts@go-wifi.co.za. You may also contact the Information Regulator (South Africa).

8. Children

The App is not directed at children.

9. Changes

We may update this page. The date at the top will change when we do.
